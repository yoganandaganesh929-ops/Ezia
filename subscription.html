Common questions

Is the Free plan really free forever?

Yes. Ezia Essential is free with no time limits. You get access to the full tracking suite — hydration, sleep, body metrics, insights, and history — at no cost.

What is the Personal AI Doctor?

The AI Doctor is your private health companion. It reads your tracked wellness data — hydration, sleep, and body metrics — and provides personalised diet plans, daily motivation, and guidance. It is designed to feel like a knowledgeable friend, not a cold chatbot.

Can I try Ezia without signing up?

Yes. Use Guest Mode on the login or landing page to explore the full Ezia Essential suite without creating an account. Your data is stored locally in your browser.

Can I cancel my Pro subscription?

Yes. You can cancel anytime. There are no lock-in periods and no hidden charges. After cancellation you retain access to all free features.
