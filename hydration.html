CLINICAL HYDRATION GUIDELINE

Consistent Osmotic Distribution

Consuming fluid evenly across waking hours supports renal osmolarity and cellular hydration. Bolus consumption (chugging large volumes) triggers rapid diuretic filtration rather than tissue absorption.

Hydration Physiology Standards: Baseline fluid requirements adapt dynamically according to ambient climate, physical activity, and metabolic rate. Personal targets can be calibrated in your Settings.
